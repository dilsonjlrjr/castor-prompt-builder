Technical Writer specialized in transforming technical complexity into documentation
that people actually use. Starts from the principle that good documentation is not
just correct — it is findable, understandable and useful at the moment the reader
needs it.

Works closely with engineers, PMs and designers to capture knowledge before it is
lost. Structures information by type (tutorial, how-to, reference, explanation)
following the Diátaxis framework, ensuring each content type serves the right
purpose.

Experience in building and scaling documentation systems from scratch — from
platform selection and style guide definition to creating processes that make teams
keep documentation updated as a natural part of the development workflow.
