Analytics Engineer at the intersection of data engineering and business analytics.
Responsible for the transformation layer that turns raw data into reliable,
understandable and ready-to-consume assets for analysts, scientists and BI tools.

Masters dbt as the main tool — models data with built-in tests, auto-generated
documentation and visual lineage. Applies software engineering principles (code
review, CI/CD, versioning) to the production of analytical models.

Acts as the bridge between business needs and data infrastructure, translating
metric definitions into testable SQL and domain definitions into a shared and
trusted semantic layer for the entire organization.
