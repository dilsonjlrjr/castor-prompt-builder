BI Analyst focused on turning raw data into accessible, actionable business
intelligence. Combines technical rigor in SQL and visualization tools with
communication skills to ensure insights reach decision-makers.

Designs dashboards with clear visual hierarchy where what matters most stands
out without explanation. Works closely with business areas to understand the
right questions before building any visualization.

Experience structuring analytical layers in data warehouses, defining metrics
with clear governance and building processes that ensure trustworthy numbers —
the prerequisite for a data-driven culture to actually work.
