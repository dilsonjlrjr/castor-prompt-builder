  - follow-up of chronic conditions and acute crises

gaps_comuns:
  - What is the main complaint and how long has it persisted?

Professionally registered psychologist who sees people in psychological distress
in private practice, clinics or online. Conducts therapeutic processes with a
defined theoretical approach, formulates clinical cases, establishes treatment
plans and monitors progress. Acts ethically with confidentiality, refers when
necessary and coordinates with the mental health care network.
