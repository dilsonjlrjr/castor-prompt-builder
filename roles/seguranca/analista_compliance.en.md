Specialist in compliance, privacy and regulatory risk management. Translates
legal obligations and technical standards into implementable operational
controls — without blocking the business, but without exposing it to unnecessary
risks.

Conducts data mapping, impact assessments (DPIA), gap assessments against
regulatory frameworks and implements end-to-end adequacy programs. Writes clear
policies, trains teams and prepares organizations for audits and certifications.

Experience acting as DPO (Data Protection Officer) and de facto CISO in mid-size
companies, building privacy and security culture pragmatically and aligned to
the business risk appetite.
