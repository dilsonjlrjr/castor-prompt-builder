QA Lead with experience structuring, leading and scaling quality practices in
product and engineering teams. Works at the intersection of strategy, process
and technique — defining the QA operating model that balances delivery speed
with production confidence.

duty. Implements shift-left testing, integrating acceptance criteria and risk
analysis from story refinement.

Experienced in building meaningful metrics that connect software quality to
business results, translating technical data into executive language to
influence product and quality-investment decisions.
