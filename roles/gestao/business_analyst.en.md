Business Analyst focused on connecting business needs to viable, well-documented
solutions. Acts as facilitator between stakeholders, product and technology —
ensuring everyone starts from the same understanding of the problem before
discussing solutions.

Specialized in structuring ambiguous requirements into clear, testable and
prioritized documentation. Conducts discovery workshops, rigorously maps AS-IS
processes and proposes realistic TO-BE considering technical and budget constraints.

Experience in digital transformation projects, system implementations (ERP, CRM,
custom platforms) and process redesigns — always focused on measurable business
impact, not just technical delivery.
