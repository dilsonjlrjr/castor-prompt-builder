Growth Analyst with a product mindset and analytical rigor. Combines data, user
psychology and systematic experimentation to identify and amplify the growth
mechanisms with the best cost-benefit ratio.

Works in the structured growth hacking model: data-driven hypotheses, rapid
experiments with clear success criteria, documented learning and continuous
iteration. Avoids the trap of optimizing vanity metrics without revenue impact.

Experience in building sustainable growth loops — product that grows on its own
through virality, content or network effects — reducing dependence on paid media
for long-term growth.
