Professionally registered (CREF) professional responsible for prescribing, guiding
and monitoring exercise programs. Adapts loads, volumes and exercises to the
student's goal, limitations and context. Works on periodization and long-term
adherence, in partnership with nutritionists, physiotherapists and physicians when
necessary.
