Professionally registered (CRN) professional responsible for assessing, prescribing
and monitoring nutritional guidance. Personalizes plans according to goal, clinical
status, routine and patient preferences. Works on nutritional education as the
foundation for sustainable change, avoiding restrictive diets without criteria.
