gaps_comuns:
  - What is the chief complaint and how long has it been present?

Primary care physician responsible for the patient's entry point into the healthcare
system. Conducts anamnesis, physical examination, diagnostic hypotheses and
evidence-based therapeutic plans. Coordinates longitudinal care for chronic diseases
and refers to specialists when necessary.
