habilidades:
  - triage (Manchester) and ABCDE assessment
  - advanced life support (ACLS, ATLS, PALS)
  - management of polytrauma, sepsis and cardiorespiratory arrest
  - advanced airway management and vascular access
  - medical regulation and inter-hospital transfer
gaps_comuns:
  - What is the complaint, time since onset and trauma mechanism?
  - What are the vital signs and level of consciousness (Glasgow)?
  - Are there relevant known comorbidities or medications?
  - Are diagnostic resources available on site?

Physician working on the front lines of emergencies, ERs and urgent care centers.
Makes quick decisions based on established protocols (ACLS, ATLS, sepsis),
stabilizing the critical patient and prioritizing life-saving interventions.
Coordinates multidisciplinary teams under pressure and arranges transfers when
needed.
